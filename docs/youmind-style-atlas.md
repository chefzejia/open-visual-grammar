## Cluster Signatures / 聚类签名

### Constrained Editorial Print

**Signature:** a published-looking frame or rule system; one dominant headline
that participates in the scene; one or two inks on paper; hard contrast; halftone,
risograph, or screen-print artifacts; whitespace is active composition.

**Variables:** subject, visual action, evidence object, ink colors, headline
length, aspect ratio, and whether type is generated or deterministically set.

**Boundary:** this is not “make it monochrome” and not a generic retro filter.
If the frame, scale rhythm, and editorial relation disappear, route back to the
pattern or choose a quieter linework pattern.

### Retro-Future Print Plate

**Signature:** one structural subject sits inside a dense, reference-anchored
print plate; etched contour lines, halftone or ink grain, and hard-edged warm/cool
planes create depth without gradients; the subject's function remains the first
read.

**Variables:** subject, concrete relation, contour density, warm/cool block
balance, dark-field versus paper-field ground, crop, and deterministic text
handling.

**Boundary:** this is not a generic science-fiction scene, spaceship preset, or
cinematic color grade. If the subject relation disappears, or the plate needs
neon glow, glossy 3D, or multiple competing machines, delete this Pattern rather
than weakening its contract.

### Handdrawn Memory Page

**Signature:** one focal subject or relation; at least roughly 40-45% breathable
paper field; thin, hesitant, or broken hand lines; 3-6 low-saturation fills with
one small bright jump; short handwritten language grows from the action or empty
space; background details are deliberately forgotten.

**Variables:** subject identity, crop or offset, caption, paper tint, accent hue,
and whether the page is a single plate or a small journal spread.

**Boundary:** do not add childish clutter, yellowed “vintage” grime, sticker
decoration, or a generic watercolor wash. The subject must remain identifiable
and the whitespace must do compositional work.

### Structural Line Reduction

**Signature:** retain the subject's key proportion, direction, structure, and
relation; build a black or dark geometric line skeleton; use stippling, dotted
texture, or restrained risograph grain for atmosphere; keep one or two source-
derived spot colors and a clear quiet focal point.

**Variables:** line density, perspective guides, spot-color targets, crop,
annotation scale, and the amount of background memory retained.

**Boundary:** this is not an exact architectural diagram and not a decorative
line-art object floating beside a title. If the source relation cannot be read,
reduce fewer details or change the visual action before adding texture.

### Pixel Silhouette Mask

**Signature:** a source-derived silhouette acts as a visual container; staircase
blocks, stepped planes, and limited color fields nest inside that contour; the
result is a modern editorial reconstruction rather than an 8-bit game screen.

**Variables:** silhouette, nested detail, block scale, palette, crop, and optional
sticker or panel border.

**Boundary:** no random pixel noise, game HUD, Minecraft-like world, or full-frame
mosaic. The pixel treatment must explain the chosen subject or action.

### Multi-Page Handdrawn Explainer

**Signature:** four to eight `9:16` pages; one claim, scene, or transition per
page; one recurring actor, object, or witness; warm near-white paper; low-
saturation watercolor or colored-pencil fills; fine handdrawn ink; sparse
collage marks; and a short, mobile-readable text hierarchy.

**Variables:** page count, progression/comparison/steps/timeline logic, anchor
identity, page beats, crop, and exact text handled by deterministic typesetting.

**Boundary:** this is a sequence with a readable argument, not a dashboard or a
sticker sheet. Do not pad a short idea with decorative pages, let an anchor change
role or direction, or use source-specific animals and costumes as the reason the
style appears stable.

### Emotional Metaphor Editorial

**Signature:** a short checked line is the first read; one Aha relation makes its
hidden feeling visible; a low-saturation paper or neutral field holds one small
high-purity semantic anchor; one or two necessary forms and active whitespace
produce a non-photorealistic editorial poster.

**Variables:** core text, emotional source context, relation type, anchor object
or mark, tiny publication information, crop, and deterministic text handling.

**Boundary:** this is not a lyric or film fan poster, an artist imitation, or a
text template with a decorative background. Do not stack metaphors, literalize
every noun, add multiple bright accents, or let source-specific IP carry the
style.

### Animal Mascot Icon

**Signature:** one credible animal silhouette carries the first read; natural
species color and anatomy remain intact; one posture, negative-space relation, or
body feature expresses the product function; shallow matte depth and a stable
square platform frame support recognition at 32 x 32 and 64 x 64.

**Variables:** species, identity anchor, function cue, pose, natural palette,
platform frame, and whether a verified glyph is required.

**Boundary:** this is not a generic fantasy creature, a mascot costume, or an
icon with a logo and prop pasted onto an animal. Do not let expression, habitat,
gloss, or a second object replace the single function cue.

## Fidelity Gate / 保真门

Review each future variant in this order:

1. **Structure before surface:** can the cluster be identified from space,
   hierarchy, and relation before texture?
2. **Subject fidelity:** did source-derived reduction preserve the identity,
   proportion, direction, or action that the score supplied?
3. **Transfer:** can a second topic use the same route without importing the
   original object's story, slogan, or coordinates?
4. **Material budget:** are paper, ink, line, pixel, or grain decisions limited
   to the cluster's signature rather than decorative accumulation?
5. **Text safety:** exact text is short and checked, or reserved for deterministic
   typesetting. No generated pseudo-Chinese or copied source slogans.
