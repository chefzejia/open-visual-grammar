# YouMind Gold-Foil Split Transfer / YouMind 纯金分屏迁移

This promotion-candidate eval tests whether the public `GPT2 x 金箔 x 转绘 x 美学提示词 x VOL.044`
method transfers beyond a daily-photo sample. It must preserve the portrait
documentary-to-flat-gold split, front-facing plane, derived dark base, and
restrained foil material without copying a source object or phrase.

本用例用于晋级前测试，判断公开 `GPT2 x 金箔 x 转绘 x 美学提示词 x VOL.044` 方法能否迁移到日常照片样本之外。
必须保留竖版纪实到平面纯金的分屏、平视承载面、提取后的深色底和克制金属材质，同时不复制来源物件或文案。

## Fixed Input Contract / 固定输入契约

Keep the same audience, `3:4` ratio, split logic, text policy, and gold material
budget across both topics. Each topic supplies one verified source record, one
recognizable subject, and one action or relation that the lower plate can reduce.

| Topic | Upper source record | Lower subject relation | Optional checked note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Repair library returns a worn tool to circulation | documentary photograph of a visibly used adjustable wrench on a workbench | open jaw aligned with a small repaired fastener | `RETURN TO USE` |
| Night ferry reconnects two shores after a service pause | documentary photograph of a ferry wake and lit terminal | wake line joins two separated shore marks | `CROSS AGAIN` |

The source records and action facts are semantic inputs. The adapter owns the
portrait split, planar gold reduction, derived base, foil texture, whitespace,
and microtype policy.

The lower gold mark must explain the supplied action or relation without becoming
a decorative luxury symbol. Do not invent a crest, jewelry, statue, or generic
gold ornament when the source contract does not provide one.

## Three-Candidate Requirement / 三候选要求

Generate exactly three candidates for each topic while keeping the score fixed:

1. `Strict Split`: equal functional upper and lower plates; the same subject and action are easiest to compare.
2. `Gold Identity Plate`: the lower gold reduction owns the first symbolic read while the upper record anchors identity.
3. `Embossed Edge Note`: the same split with one short checked note near the lower edge; no extra copy or decorative frame.

All candidates use the same front-facing plane, one dark derived base hue, matte-to-
soft gold foil, and active whitespace. Candidate differences must test structure,
not hue rotation, extra props, or a new material.

## Pass Signals / 通过信号

- Upper documentary record and lower flat-gold reduction read as one subject or action, not two stacked stories.
- The lower gold mark remains recognizable without the optional note.
- Gold is planar, matte-to-soft, and tactile; no glossy 3D object or luxury ornament replaces the subject.
- The lower base is one clean darkened hue derived from the source, with meaningful whitespace.
- The same adapter and three candidate families work for both unrelated topics.
- No copied source phrase, object-specific crest, logo, or fixed decorative coordinate is introduced.

## Fail Signals / 失败信号

- The lower half becomes a shiny statue, jewelry object, or generic premium logo.
- The upper and lower halves lose their shared subject or action.
- Gold texture, contrast, or pseudo-type carries recognition instead of the source relation.
- The dark base becomes a gradient, noisy luxury backdrop, or multi-color palette.
- The two topics require different split systems, or the candidates differ only by color and crop.
