# YouMind Graphic Fashion Avatar Fidelity / YouMind 时尚稚拙头像保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-graphic-avatar-transfer.md`. Judge identity
reduction and small-size legibility before rewarding fashion mood or texture.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-graphic-avatar-transfer.md` 配套使用。先判断身份减法和小尺寸可读性，再评价时尚气质或材质。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- Hair silhouette, face shape, and expression jointly identify the supplied
  avatar without a label.
- One optional accessory is subordinate to the silhouette and remains source-
  grounded.
- Rough marker or dry-brush edges are controlled, with restrained watercolor
  color on a clean warm-white field.
- The avatar stays recognizable at 64 x 64 and 32 x 32 without adding text or
  decorative props.
- Both unrelated topics use the same reduction and material budget.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- Fashion styling is attractive, but the identity anchors could belong to any
  character.
- Rough edges, watercolor, or white space are present only as a generic filter.
- Extra clothing, props, or a scene carries recognition instead of the face and
  hair.
- The avatar reads only at full size and collapses at thumbnail scale.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer name the hair, face, and expression anchors without reading
   the prompt?
2. Does the one accessory clarify identity, or is it functioning as a logo or
   costume shortcut?
3. Would the same silhouette-first reduction survive a third unrelated avatar?
4. Are the three candidates structural tests rather than palette or crop swaps?
5. Is the artifact really an app icon, or does the evidence require a separate
   profile-avatar registry kind before an adapter can be promoted?
