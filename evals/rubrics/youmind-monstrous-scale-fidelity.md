# YouMind Monstrous-Scale Fidelity / YouMind 巨物尺度保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-monstrous-scale-transfer.md`. Judge the
scale relation and witness action before rewarding cinematic atmosphere.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-monstrous-scale-transfer.md` 配套使用，先判断尺度关系和见证者动作，再评价电影化氛围。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- One world silhouette exceeds the frame and controls the first read.
- A tiny witness has a concrete action that reveals or measures the world.
- Scale is legible from relative size and crop, not only from adjectives.
- A single path or relation links witness and world without adding a second story.
- Reduced print treatment still preserves the scale contrast on both topics.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The subject is large but fully contained, so the frame no longer feels exceeded.
- The witness is present but has no role beyond proving a human exists.
- Fog, stars, glow, or grain creates mood while the world relation remains generic.
- One topic succeeds only because it resembles a fantasy concept cover.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer name the world, the witness action, and their scale relation without reading the prompt?
2. Does cropping make the world feel larger, or merely incomplete?
3. Would the same witness logic survive a third world silhouette?
4. Are the three candidates structural variants rather than atmosphere or palette swaps?
5. Is the evidence sufficient for a seed Pattern, or should this remain a promotion candidate?
