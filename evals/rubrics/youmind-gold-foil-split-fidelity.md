# YouMind Gold-Foil Split Fidelity / YouMind 纯金分屏保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-gold-foil-split-transfer.md`. Judge the
shared documentary relation and planar reduction before rewarding luxury mood.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-gold-foil-split-transfer.md` 配套使用。先判断纪实关系与平面化是否成立，再评价奢雅气质。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- The portrait `3:4` frame visibly contains an upper source record and a lower gold reduction with equal functional intent.
- The lower plate preserves one recognizable contour, identity cue, or action without needing the note.
- Gold reads as flat pressed foil or thin hammered metal with restrained matte-to-soft luster, edge lift, and small breaks.
- The lower base is one clean darkened hue derived from supplied evidence; whitespace remains active.
- Optional microtype is short, checked, low-contrast, and subordinate to the image relation.
- Both topics and all three candidate families preserve the same split and material budget.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- Gold color and paper feel premium, but the lower subject could be any generic emblem.
- The upper half is a separate photograph and the lower half an unrelated decoration.
- Specular highlights, perspective, or thick extrusion make the gold a 3D object.
- A title, pseudo-lettering, or luxury motif carries recognition when the contour does not.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can the reviewer name the shared subject or action after hiding the optional note?
2. Does the lower plate still read as planar when color and texture are muted?
3. Is the dark base traceable to the source rather than a generic luxury gradient?
4. Would the same split survive a third non-portrait topic without a new art direction?
5. Are the three candidates structural tests rather than hue, crop, or prop swaps?

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** shared subject/action, clear split, planar gold, derived base, and bounded text all hold across both topics and candidate families.
- **Revise:** recognition transfers but one material, text, or split boundary is missing; record one adapter change.
- **Reject:** the result depends on generic luxury symbols, glossy 3D gold, copied coordinates, or text that substitutes for the image relation.
