# YouMind Box-Operator Fidelity / YouMind 小盒小工保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-box-operator-transfer.md`. Judge the
worker-action relation before rewarding the character's charm.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-box-operator-transfer.md` 配套使用。先判断小工动作与证据物件的关系，再评价角色是否讨喜。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- One closed box-like worker remains stable in silhouette, direction, and role.
- The worker performs one concrete verb that changes the evidence object or path.
- The judgment or risk can be understood from the physical state change before
  labels are read.
- Pure white space, thin black line, and bounded orange/red/blue notes preserve
  the whiteboard parent grammar.
- Both unrelated topics use the same local one-action composition.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The worker is recognizable, but the object state never changes.
- Labels explain the judgment while the scene is only decorative.
- The output needs several characters, props, or a full system map.
- Cute expression or color overwhelms the serious working action.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer point to the worker's one verb and its before/after consequence?
2. Does the evidence object or path carry the article judgment without a
   paragraph of labels?
3. Would the same closed-worker role survive a third unrelated workflow?
4. Are the three candidates action-structure tests rather than color or camera
   swaps?
5. Does this remain an adapter of `whiteboard-operator-sketch`, or does the
   recurring box silhouette require a separate artifact route?
