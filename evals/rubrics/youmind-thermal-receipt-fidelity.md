# YouMind Thermal Receipt Fidelity / YouMind 热敏小票保真度

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-thermal-receipt-transfer.md`. It judges the
record method and factual discipline, not generic attractiveness or image-model
capability.

## Strong Signals / 强信号

### 1. Record Structure

- One continuous receipt or ticket silhouette owns the canvas.
- The identity anchor appears first and the remaining fields follow a stable order.
- The three routes differ in line count and whitespace, not in underlying grammar.

### 2. Factual Safety

- Dates, places, names, numbers, and measurements are traceable to the fixed input.
- Missing fields are omitted rather than filled with plausible values.
- The quote is short, verified, and subordinate to the record.

### 3. Material And Density

- Warm off-white paper, dark print, slight banding, and uneven heat coverage stay restrained.
- Whitespace remains visible when the record is sparse.
- The result does not use coffee stains, sepia grime, glossy plastic, or a photographic prop.

### 4. Transfer

- The same method works for a travel failure, a personal adjustment, and an operational interruption.
- Removing thermal adjectives still leaves the same vertical record structure.
- No creator name, source slogan, or exact reference composition is copied.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The image feels nostalgic but has no stable field order.
- A fake barcode, total, or timestamp is used as decoration.
- Candidates are palette swaps or camera changes rather than route changes.
- The quote is legible but the factual record is too small to verify.

## Review Questions / 复盘问题

1. Can the record be scanned before the paper texture is inspected?
2. Which exact fact anchors the receipt, and where is its source in the input?
3. Does the quote create a pause without changing the record's job?
4. Is sparse content allowed to remain sparse?
5. Does each route preserve the same method after color and material words are removed?

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** record structure, factual safety, material discipline, and cross-topic transfer all hold.
- **Revise:** the record is sound but one route, text, or density constraint needs a targeted adapter correction.
- **Reject:** the result invents facts, loses the receipt contract, or requires an unsupported composition.
