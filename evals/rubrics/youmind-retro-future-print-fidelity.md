# YouMind Retro-Future Print Fidelity / YouMind 复古未来印刷保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-retro-future-print-transfer.md`. Judge the
subject relation, block structure, and print material before rewarding a
science-fiction mood or surface detail.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-retro-future-print-transfer.md` 配套使用，先判断
主体关系、色块结构和印刷材质，再评价科幻氛围或表面细节。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- One supplied structural subject remains clear and performs the supplied
  relation.
- Hard-edged warm/cool blocks create a readable hierarchy instead of a gradient.
- Etched contours, halftone or ink grain, and small registration irregularities
  are visible but subordinate to the subject.
- The print plate works on both topics and in the dark-field, split-plane, and
  paper-reversal candidates.
- Removing “science-fiction” from the prompt does not collapse the visual method.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The image looks retro only because of orange/navy color, while contour density
  and relation are generic.
- Mechanical detail accumulates without explaining what the subject does.
- The paper reversal loses the plate hierarchy and becomes a loose sketch or
  watercolor image.
- One topic succeeds only because it can be mistaken for a spaceship or control
  panel.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer name the structural subject and its concrete relation without
   reading the style words?
2. Do the warm/cool blocks separate meaningful planes, or are they merely a
   palette swap?
3. Does print grain support the contour system at feed size rather than hide it?
4. Are the three candidates structural variants rather than lens, glow, or hue
   changes?
5. Is the evidence sufficient for a new print-plate Pattern, or should this stay
   material evidence alongside the existing retro-pixel editorial route?
