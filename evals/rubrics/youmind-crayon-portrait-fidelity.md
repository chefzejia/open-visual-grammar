# YouMind Crayon Portrait Fidelity / YouMind 蜡笔肖像保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-crayon-portrait-transfer.md`. Judge identity
reduction and paper behavior before rewarding warmth, fashion mood, or surface
detail.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-crayon-portrait-transfer.md` 配套使用。先判断身份减法和纸面行为，再评价温度、时尚气质或表面细节。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- Face shape, hair pattern, expression, and one or two supplied details identify the person without a label.
- White drawing paper, dry rubbing, broken pressure, and open edges feel like a live study rather than a filter.
- Most areas remain paper-white, with a restrained warm hair range and a few structural coral, brick, blue, or peacock cues.
- A one-color side wash supports the shoulders and stops at the contour.
- Both unrelated topics keep the same `3:4` composition and material budget.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The portrait is attractive but the identity anchors could belong to any person.
- Crayon marks are present only as generic grain, blur, or a yellowed paper filter.
- Extra clothing, props, background, or text replaces face and hair recognition.
- The result is legible only at full size or becomes a caricature when the expression changes.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer name the face, hair, and expression anchors without reading the prompt?
2. Does the paper field remain open around the head and lower page?
3. Is every accent color tied to a supplied structural plane or shadow cue?
4. Would the same identity-first reduction survive a third unrelated portrait?
5. Are the three candidates structural tests rather than palette, crop, or accessory swaps?
