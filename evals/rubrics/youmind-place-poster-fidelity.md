# YouMind Place-Poster Fidelity / YouMind 地点海报保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-place-poster-transfer.md`. Judge place
recognition and poster structure before rewarding color or nostalgia.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-place-poster-transfer.md` 配套使用，先判断地点识别与海报结构，再评价配色或怀旧感。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- One short place name participates in the frame as a dominant, integrated type
  block or has a reserved deterministic region.
- One supplied landmark or landmark cluster remains recognizable without the
  title, and one geographic cue explains the place action.
- Three to five high-saturation spot colors are source-aware, limited, and
  semantically useful.
- Shaky contour, collage planes, screenprint overlap, uneven coverage, paper,
  and active whitespace form one coherent poster system.
- All three composition families preserve the same adapter method and the same
  place facts.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- A large city name is present, but the image could depict any city.
- Landmark detail is attractive but has no relation to the supplied geographic
  cue.
- The output looks like a postcard or sticker sheet with decorative icons.
- Grain, overprint, and saturated color carry the style while the frame and
  hierarchy disappear.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can the reviewer identify the place relation after hiding the title?
2. Do the spot colors point to supplied evidence or merely create a travel mood?
3. Are the three candidates structural variants inside one poster system?
4. Would the adapter survive a different city and landmark without inventing a
   new art direction?

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** place, landmark, cue, poster frame, and bounded ink system all hold
  across both topics and all three candidate families.
- **Revise:** recognition transfers but one material, text, or density boundary
  is missing; record one adapter change.
- **Reject:** the result depends on generic tourism symbols, copied coordinates,
  invented facts, or a title that substitutes for the image relation.
