# Source Picture-Book Sequence Fidelity / 来源绘本跨页序列保真

Use this rubric with
`cases/source-picture-book-sequence-transfer.md`. Judge page grammar and
transfer safety before rewarding cuteness, polish, or a particular illustration
surface.

本 rubric 与 `cases/source-picture-book-sequence-transfer.md` 配套。先判断页面语法和迁移安全，再评价可爱、精致或某一种插画表面。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

### Sequence

- Every page advances one verb-led beat and the reading direction is obvious.
- The chosen family (progression, reveal, or comparison) matches the source argument.
- Quiet and busy fields alternate for a reason; a reveal is not decorative noise.

### Anchor

- The supplied actor, object, or witness remains role-stable, recognizable, and directionally coherent.
- Concrete evidence appears where it explains the argument rather than spreading as generic props.

### Material And Density

- One material branch remains consistent and leaves enough breathing room at mobile size.
- Short text attaches to the scene; exact long copy is deterministic and does not carry the image alone.

### Transfer And Safety

- Both unrelated topics pass without source characters, costumes, lettering, publisher marks, or copied copy.
- The sequence still works when material words are removed from the score.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The pages look handmade but have no causal or temporal relation.
- A recurring anchor hides the missing argument instead of clarifying it.
- Paper texture, color, or collage marks carry the style while page order collapses.
- The three candidates differ only by hue, crop, or decorative props.

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** Sequence, Anchor, and Transfer And Safety strong signals are all present; no critical failure appears.
- **Revise:** the sequence is sound but one page, text zone, or material budget needs a targeted correction.
- **Reject:** the result depends on copied source identity, loses page progression, or requires an unsupported composition family.
