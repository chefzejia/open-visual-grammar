# YouMind Portrait-Quote Fidelity / YouMind 人像台词海报保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-portrait-quote-transfer.md`. Judge the
portrait-to-text relation and transfer boundary before rewarding cinematic mood.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-portrait-quote-transfer.md` 配套使用，先判断人像与文字关系及迁移边界，再评价电影氛围。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- The portrait's identity anchors, pose, and physical action carry the first read
  even when the quote is hidden.
- A short quote sits in a protected lower zone with a clear type hierarchy; it
  never covers the face or competes with the portrait.
- One scene cue supports the quote's emotional intent while remaining subordinate
  to portrait and text.
- Portrait scale, lower-zone behavior, vertical framing, and restrained scene
  density remain stable across both topics and all three candidate routes.
- Exact wording is checked or handed to deterministic post-layout; no copied
  slogan or source person's likeness is needed.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The page looks cinematic, but portrait identity and quote could be swapped
  independently without changing the composition.
- A lower caption exists, yet it is too small, too long, or too close to the face
  to function as designed text.
- The scene cue becomes a full film set, stock wallpaper, or generic color grade.
- The three candidates are color treatments rather than structural tests.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Does the portrait still communicate the supplied action with the quote hidden?
2. Is the lower text zone protected and legible at feed size?
3. Does the same portrait-to-text relation survive a different subject and scene?
4. Is the atmosphere owned by one scene cue, or is it compensating for a weak
   layout?
5. Is the evidence sufficient for a new Pattern, or should this remain a
   promotion candidate?

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** identity, action, protected quote zone, scene restraint, and
  cross-topic transfer all hold with safe text handling.
- **Revise:** the composition transfers but one identity, typography, or runtime
  constraint is missing; record one targeted adapter or post-layout change.
- **Reject:** the result depends on copied likeness, long or broken text, generic
  film wallpaper, or an unsupported composition. Keep it out of OVG.
