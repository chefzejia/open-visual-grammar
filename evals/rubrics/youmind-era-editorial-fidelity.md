# YouMind Fixed-Era Editorial Fidelity / YouMind 固定年代编辑视觉保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-era-editorial-transfer.md`. Judge whether
the historical branch controls a complete visual system and transfers to new
content, not whether the output merely looks old or attractive.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-era-editorial-transfer.md` 配套使用，判断历史分支是否真正控制了
完整视觉系统并能迁移到新内容，不评价“像不像旧海报”或单张图是否漂亮。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

### 1. Era System

- The fixed 1940s branch is legible through coordinated limited-color planes,
  display-type behavior, asymmetrical or block geometry, woodcut/print marks,
  paper, and active whitespace.
- The era choices reinforce one another; no single grain or tint is doing all
  the historical work.
- The result reads as a newly authored editorial artifact, not a reconstruction
  of a known poster or a generic retro preset.

### 2. Subject And Action

- The supplied subject, physical action, and evidence object remain identifiable
  without the title.
- The evidence object explains the same correction or reader question rather
  than introducing a second story.
- The first read remains recoverable at thumbnail size in all three candidates.

### 3. Transfer And Ownership

- Both unrelated topics keep the same target era, typography hierarchy, palette
  budget, geometry, material, and whitespace behavior.
- No historical slogan, celebrity, uniform, flag, creator name, or exact source
  coordinate is copied.
- The article owns semantics; the era route owns composition, density, type,
  material, and emotional temperature.

### 4. Text And Material Safety

- Exact text is short and checked, or handed to deterministic typesetting.
- Limited inks and print marks remain subordinate to the action; distress does
  not hide missing structure or unreadable evidence.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The image feels nostalgic only because of beige paper, scratches, or a filter.
- A convincing display face is present, but geometry and whitespace do not carry
  the same branch.
- One topic becomes a period scene while the other is a modern poster with a
  token woodcut texture.
- The title explains the action because the visual relation is too weak to read.
- The three candidates vary hue or crop without testing a real layout decision.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. If the paper tint and grain are removed, do palette, type, geometry, and
   whitespace still identify the fixed 1940s branch?
2. Can a reviewer point to the physical action and evidence object before reading
   the title?
3. Does the same era system survive both topics without a copied historical
   prop, slogan, or composition?
4. Are the three candidates distinct structural solutions within one branch?
5. Should this remain a promotion candidate, or is there enough reviewed
   cross-topic evidence for an era-aware Pattern?

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** all strong signals in Era System, Subject And Action, and Transfer
  And Ownership are present; no critical fail signal appears.
- **Revise:** the visual argument transfers, but one era invariant or runtime
  constraint is missing. Record one targeted adapter or anti-pattern change.
- **Reject:** the result depends on copied historical details, a generic retro
  filter, unreadable text, or an unsupported composition. Keep it out of OVG.
