# YouMind Animal Mascot Fidelity / YouMind 动物吉祥物保真度

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-animal-mascot-transfer.md`. Judge whether
the animal mark carries the product function, not whether it looks cute or
highly rendered.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-animal-mascot-transfer.md` 配套，判断动物标记是否承载产品功能，不评价是否可爱或渲染是否精细。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

### 1. Species And Recognition

- The animal's anatomy, silhouette, and natural color identity are credible.
- One identity anchor remains visible at 32 x 32 and 64 x 64.
- The function is recoverable from one posture, negative-space relation, or body feature.

### 2. Depth And Platform

- Two or three shallow relief or overlap levels support the mark.
- One soft light direction and matte or satin surfaces keep depth subordinate.
- iOS and macOS framing preserve the same silhouette and identity anchor.

### 3. Transfer And Safety

- Three candidates test emblem, pose relation, and body-feature routes rather than color swaps.
- No logo, badge, costume, prop, copied character, or wordmark is needed for recognition.
- Removing species name and material adjectives leaves the same function-bearing icon grammar.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The animal is attractive but species identity or function is generic.
- A badge, tool, label, or costume carries the product meaning.
- Gloss, heavy extrusion, or photoreal fur overwhelms the silhouette.
- Platform crops change the identity anchor.
- Candidates differ only by hue, lighting, or camera angle.

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** all strong signals in Species And Recognition, Depth And Platform, and Transfer And Safety are present; no critical failure appears.
- **Revise:** the animal and function are sound but one cue, crop, or depth rule needs a targeted adapter change.
- **Reject:** the result depends on copied source details, loses small-size function, or requires a second story outside the Pattern.
