# YouMind Mist-Blue Metaphor Fidelity / YouMind 雾蓝单隐喻保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-mist-blue-metaphor-transfer.md`. Judge the
text-metaphor relationship before rewarding palette or print grain.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-mist-blue-metaphor-transfer.md` 配套使用，先判断文字—隐喻关系，再评价
配色与印刷颗粒。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- The short checked title is the first read and remains legible at feed size.
- One visual relation visibly changes, exposes, compresses, or distances the
  title's meaning; no second story is needed.
- Mist blue and off-white form the dominant field, with at most one warm accent
  tied to the semantic hinge.
- Local spray or print grain supports the relation while generous 16:9 whitespace
  remains active.
- The same text-first system transfers to both unrelated topics and all three
  composition families.
- Exact text is checked or deterministic; hiding the text does not leave only a
  generic mood image.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The title is readable, but the surrounding image could accompany any sentence.
- A misty palette and grain are convincing while the relation is generic fog,
  circuitry, or decorative objects.
- The warm accent and texture compete with the title or consume the whitespace.
- Candidates are palette swaps or crop variants rather than structural tests.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Is the title still the first read when the image is viewed at feed size?
2. Can the hidden tension be named from one relation, without a second metaphor?
3. Does the same relation survive when palette and grain are removed?
4. Is the warm accent semantic and limited to one color?
5. Should a failure become a Pattern boundary, adapter change, or text-handling
   rule?

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** text-first hierarchy, one relation, restrained palette, active
  whitespace, safe text handling, and cross-topic transfer all hold.
- **Revise:** the metaphor transfers but one relation, palette, or runtime text
  constraint is missing; record one targeted adapter adjustment.
- **Reject:** the output is decorative text-on-background, generic AI fog,
  multi-metaphor clutter, unreadable copy, copied source material, or an
  unsupported composition. Keep it out of OVG.
