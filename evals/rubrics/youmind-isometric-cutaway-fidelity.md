# YouMind Isometric Cutaway Fidelity / YouMind 等距剖切保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-isometric-cutaway-transfer.md`. Judge the
section relationship and support logic before rewarding miniature detail or
surface polish.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-isometric-cutaway-transfer.md` 配套使用，先判断剖切关系和支撑逻辑，再评价微缩细节或表面精致度。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- Isometric axes and section planes make the object readable without labels.
- A support or survey frame wraps, braces, reconnects, or measures a named relation.
- The cutaway reveals a meaningful dependency rather than a decorative interior.
- White paper, restrained color, and short labels create a catalog-like hierarchy.
- The method survives both non-building topics after source architecture is removed.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The object is recognizable but the opened section has no explanatory role.
- Scaffold lines look precise yet do not touch or support the failing relation.
- Labels and tiny parts carry the explanation that the geometry should provide.
- The result resembles a toy diorama or glossy product render.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer point to the cutaway, the dependency, and the support action?
2. Would the same isometric construction work on another object with different layers?
3. Is the frame structurally necessary, or could it be deleted without changing the story?
4. Are the three candidates distinct structural solutions rather than camera changes?
5. Is there enough cross-topic evidence to create a seed Pattern, or should this remain a promotion candidate?
