# YouMind Dual-Focal Swirl Fidelity / YouMind 双焦点漩涡线描保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-dual-focal-swirl-transfer.md`. Judge the
spatial method and transfer quality, not the attractiveness of a single color
palette or the amount of illustration detail.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-dual-focal-swirl-transfer.md` 配套使用，判断空间方法和迁移质量，不评价单张图的配色漂亮程度或细节数量。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- Two supplied anchors remain distinct and jointly explain the first read.
- Swirl or circular line rhythm creates an intentional route between them.
- Dense marks gather around meaningful structure while open gouache field protects breathing space.
- Flat complementary blocks remain limited and clarify the relation.
- The method reads on both topics after urban-night words and source story are removed.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The two anchors are present but their relation is only implied by the prompt.
- Lines are attractive yet evenly distributed, so density has no hierarchy.
- Watercolor wash or complementary hue carries most of the style without a spatial argument.
- One topic works only because it resembles the source's city composition.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer point to both anchors and the visual path between them?
2. Does the open field make the relation easier to read, or is it merely empty background?
3. Would the same line rhythm survive if the anchors changed again?
4. Are the three candidates different structural solutions rather than palette swaps?
5. Should this remain a promotion candidate, or is there enough unrelated-topic evidence for a seed Pattern?
