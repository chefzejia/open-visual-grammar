# YouMind Cognitive Contrast Card Fidelity / YouMind 认知反差卡保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-cognitive-contrast-card-transfer.md`. Judge
the comparison logic and recurring anchor before rewarding illustration charm.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-cognitive-contrast-card-transfer.md` 配套使用。先判断对比逻辑和重复锚点，再评价插画气质。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- The same anchor and card frame recur without identity or direction drift.
- Each card shows an early intuitive state and a later fuller state in concrete
  paired scenes.
- The later context changes the judgment; it is not merely a second caption.
- Text is short, checked, and subordinate to the two-state scene relation.
- The five-card sequence remains legible on a phone and works for both topics.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- A stable character is present, but every card tells a different story.
- The contrast is expressed only with color, mood, or a longer sentence.
- The visual anchor changes pose or role enough to look like a new character.
- Decorative stickers, badges, or platform cues become the apparent structure.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer identify the early intuition, the fuller judgment, and the
   visual evidence for the change on every card?
2. Does the recurring anchor stay role-stable and recognizable at phone size?
3. Would the same five-card comparison survive a third unrelated life topic?
4. Are the three candidates different sequence structures rather than palette or
   typography swaps?
5. Does the method belong as a `narrative-journal-infographic` adapter, or does
   it require a separate social-card artifact route?
