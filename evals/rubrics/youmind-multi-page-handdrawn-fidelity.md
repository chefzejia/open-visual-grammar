# YouMind Multi-Page Handdrawn Fidelity / YouMind 多页手绘保真度

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-multi-page-handdrawn-transfer.md`. Judge
the sequence method, not the charm of the characters or the polish of the
rendering.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-multi-page-handdrawn-transfer.md` 配套，判断页面序列方法，不评价角色是否可爱或渲染是否华丽。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

### 1. Sequence Structure

- Every page has one readable claim, scene, or transition.
- Progression, comparison, steps, or timeline is visible in page order.
- The sequence reaches a supplied conclusion rather than ending on decoration.

### 2. Anchor And Subject

- The recurring actor, object, or witness keeps its role, silhouette, direction, and palette.
- Concrete evidence appears in the page where it explains the argument; it is not spread as generic props.
- Three candidates vary page composition within the same sequence family and score.

### 3. Material And Density

- Warm paper, low-saturation fills, fine handdrawn ink, and sparse collage marks form one bounded system.
- Whitespace and text hierarchy remain useful at `9:16` mobile size.
- Tape, notes, arrows, and clips clarify a page relation or stay absent; they do not become scrapbook filler.

### 4. Transfer And Safety

- The method works for both fixed topics without pet-specific characters or costumes.
- Exact copy is short and checked or reserved for deterministic typesetting.
- No dashboard, mascot branding, source slogan, creator name, or private asset is copied.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- Pages look handmade but have no readable sequence.
- A stable character hides the fact that the argument is missing.
- Color, tape, or paper texture carries the style while page hierarchy collapses.
- Candidates differ only by palette or decorative stickers.

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** all strong signals in Sequence Structure, Anchor And Subject, and Transfer And Safety are present; no critical failure appears.
- **Revise:** the argument and sequence are sound but one page, text zone, or material budget needs a targeted adapter change.
- **Reject:** the result depends on copied source characters, loses page progression, or requires an unsupported composition.
