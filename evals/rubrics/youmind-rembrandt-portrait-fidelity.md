# YouMind Adaptive Rembrandt Portrait Fidelity / YouMind 自适应伦勃朗肖像保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-rembrandt-portrait-transfer.md`. Judge
identity and physical light before rewarding cinematic mood.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-rembrandt-portrait-transfer.md` 配套使用，先判断身份保留和物理布光，
再评价电影感。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- Identity anchors, age or breed, expression, and posture carry the first read.
- The key-light direction, shadow edge, and facial or animal structure agree;
  the method adapts instead of forcing a stock Rembrandt triangle.
- A smooth charcoal-to-black studio field stays quiet and consistent across
  candidates, with no grain, smoke, glow, banding, or synthetic texture.
- Source ratio, hair, ears, muzzle, hands, and shoulders are protected; any
  editorial reframing extends only the clean background.
- The same identity-first light relation transfers across the person and pet
  topics without copied likeness, crop, or coordinate.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The portrait looks polished, but any stock face or pet could replace the
  subject without changing the image.
- “Cinematic” is supplied by a heavy grade, rim light, fog, or vignette rather
  than by a plausible key-light relation.
- The background is dark, yet noise, paper texture, or glow competes with the
  subject.
- Three candidates are contrast or color variants instead of structural tests.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can the subject and identity anchors be named with the light hidden?
2. Does the shadow relation follow the actual face, fur, ears, muzzle, or posture?
3. Does the same method work for both a human and an animal subject?
4. Does any crop or background extension protect identity-bearing parts?
5. Is there enough reviewed evidence for a photographic Pattern, or should this
   remain a promotion candidate?

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** identity, plausible structure-aware light, clean studio field, ratio
  handling, and cross-topic transfer all hold with no critical fail signal.
- **Revise:** the visual method transfers, but one lighting, identity, or runtime
  constraint is missing; record one targeted adapter or post-layout change.
- **Reject:** the result depends on generic stock identity, forced shadows,
  cinematic texture, unsafe crop, copied likeness, or unsupported composition.
  Keep it out of OVG.
