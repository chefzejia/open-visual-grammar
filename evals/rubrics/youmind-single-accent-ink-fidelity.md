# YouMind Single-Accent Editorial Ink Fidelity / YouMind 单强调色编辑墨线保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-single-accent-ink-transfer.md`. Judge the
visual relation and accent budget before rewarding handmade texture.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-single-accent-ink-transfer.md` 配套使用，先判断视觉关系和强调色预算，
再评价手绘材质。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- The subject, action, and evidence remain readable from heavy black contours
  before halftone or text is inspected.
- Halftone dots stay local and subordinate to a subject, shadow, or evidence
  plane; the paper field remains near-white and open.
- Exactly one semantic accent color is traceable to the score and stable across
  all three candidates.
- The chosen vignette, pair, or text-zone family remains coherent across both
  unrelated topics.
- Exact copy is short and checked or handed to deterministic layout; hiding text
  does not erase the visual argument.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The page feels handmade, but the contour is generic and the action could be
  replaced without changing the image.
- Fine dots and an accent are present, yet they act as a uniform filter or palette
  showcase rather than evidence.
- A clean text area exists, but it carries the relation that the drawing should
  show.
- Candidates are palette swaps or paper-texture variants.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can the action and evidence be named with the halftone and text hidden?
2. Is there exactly one semantic accent, and where does it come from?
3. Does local halftone leave enough active whitespace for a fast first read?
4. Does the same structure transfer to the second unrelated topic?
5. Should a failure become an adapter boundary or anti-pattern?

## Decision Rule / 决策规则

- **Pass:** contour-led relation, local halftone, one accent, whitespace, text
  handling, and cross-topic transfer all hold with no critical fail signal.
- **Revise:** the route works but one material, density, or text constraint is
  missing; record one targeted adapter adjustment.
- **Reject:** the result depends on generic vector art, multi-color decoration,
  full-frame noise, unreadable action, copied brand material, or unsupported
  composition. Keep it out of OVG.
