# YouMind Spatial-Word Poster Fidelity / YouMind 空间大字海报保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-spatial-word-poster-transfer.md`. Judge the
word-space relation and action before rewarding impact or typography polish.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-spatial-word-poster-transfer.md` 配套使用。先判断文字—空间关系和互动，再评价冲击力或字体精致度。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- The short word is the first read and remains legible at feed size.
- A wall, bridge, room, or other spatial skeleton is visibly derived from that
  word's meaning.
- One actor or object changes, supports, crosses, opens, or exposes the
  word-space through a concrete action.
- The relation survives after texture, lighting, and secondary marks are
  removed.
- Both unrelated topics use one relation and a restrained editorial surface.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The text is large, but the surrounding structure is a generic scene.
- The actor illustrates the topic without changing the word's meaning.
- Typography, symbols, or atmospheric effects carry the argument instead of the
  spatial skeleton.
- The method works for one abstract word but collapses for another topic.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer name the word-space relation without reading the prompt?
2. Does the actor's action change the meaning, or is it merely present?
3. Would the same spatial skeleton survive a third unrelated short word?
4. Are the three candidates structural variants rather than font, color, or
   camera swaps?
5. Does this belong as an adapter of `semantic-concept-poster`, or does it need
   a separate typographic artifact route?
