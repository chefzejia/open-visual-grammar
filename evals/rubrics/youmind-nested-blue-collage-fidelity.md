# YouMind Nested Blue Collage Fidelity / YouMind 中蓝嵌合拼贴保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-nested-blue-collage-transfer.md`. Judge the
nested layout and subject legibility before rewarding blue color or street mood.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-nested-blue-collage-transfer.md` 配套使用。先判断嵌合版式和主体可读性，再评价蓝色或街头气质。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- Outer wrinkled paper, centered blue field, and clipped inner paper pieces are
  visibly distinct and ordered.
- One supplied subject silhouette carries the first read, with halftone texture
  acting as a reduction rather than noise.
- The handwritten orbit and one utility cue are sparse, checked, and subordinate
  to the subject.
- Torn edges feel cut and clipped at the blue boundary, not like random sticker
  debris or soft digital masking.
- Both topics share the same layout and material budget without relying on the
  source's exact phrase or color value.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The blue rectangle is attractive, but the three-layer construction is unclear.
- Typography or barcode decoration carries recognition instead of the subject.
- Paper texture is a generic overlay and the torn pieces do not meet a boundary.
- The output becomes glossy 3D, a sticker sheet, or a dense magazine spread.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer point to all three layers without reading the prompt?
2. Does the subject remain identifiable if the handwritten orbit and utility cue
   are removed?
3. Would the same nested relation survive a third non-portrait topic?
4. Are the three candidates structural tests rather than color or crop swaps?
5. Is this a reusable image-cover method, or only a fixed blue template tied to
   one source subject and phrase?
