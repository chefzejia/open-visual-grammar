# YouMind City-Observation Poster Fidelity / YouMind 城市观察海报保真

Use this rubric with `cases/youmind-city-observation-poster-transfer.md`. Judge
identity and everyday observation before rewarding travel mood.

本 rubric 与 `cases/youmind-city-observation-poster-transfer.md` 配套使用。先判断身份与日常观察，再评价旅行气质。

## Strong Signals / 强信号

- The supplied character is recognizable and role-stable in both places.
- One everyday city action carries the emotional observation without a landmark
  checklist.
- Giant title, character, and local cue share one vertical editorial hierarchy.
- Blue/cyan linework, limited palette, and screenprint texture are consistent
  and subordinate to the action.
- The same method survives both places after source branding is removed.

## Weak Signals / 弱信号

- The city name is clear, but the scene could belong anywhere.
- A landmark or skyline does the work that the character and daily action should
  do.
- The character identity drifts while the poster surface stays consistent.
- Screenprint texture or color becomes a generic filter.

## Operator Questions / 操作者问题

1. Can a reviewer identify the character and everyday action without reading the
   title?
2. Does the city cue come from an ordinary observation rather than a landmark
   list?
3. Would the same character-led relation survive a third unrelated place?
4. Are the three candidates structural variants rather than title, color, or
   crop swaps?
5. Does this remain a `minimal-handdrawn-linework` adapter rather than a new
   travel-poster Pattern?
