# Image Generation Adapter / 生图 Adapter

Use the Pattern's print system to make a future-facing editorial argument; do
not turn the subject into science-fiction scenery.

用本 Pattern 的印刷系统承载面向未来的编辑论点，不把主体改成科幻场景。

```text
Create {{ARTIFACT_KIND}} for {{AUDIENCE}} at {{ASPECT_RATIO}}.

The upstream visual argument is authoritative:
- First read: {{FIRST_READ}}
- Structural subject: {{STRUCTURAL_SUBJECT}}
- Concrete relation: {{CONCRETE_RELATION}}
- Text handling: {{TEXT_HANDLING}}

Use the Retro-Future Print Editorial Pattern. Organize one supplied structural
subject and one concrete relation with dense etched contours, local halftone,
visible print grain, and small registration irregularity. Use hard-edged warm
and cool planes to establish meaningful hierarchy. Pick Etched Field, Split
Plate, or Paper Reversal according to the argument; the route changes structure,
not the art direction.

Keep the plate authored, tactile, and non-cinematic. The subject and relation
must read before grain. Use limited color blocks, no gradients or chrome, and
reserve exact type for deterministic post-layout. “Retro-future” may affect
print geometry and contrast but does not authorize spaceships, alien lore,
neon cyberpunk, or copied source layouts.

Avoid decorative machinery, glow, smooth 3D, photographic lighting, multiple
equal subjects, random text, logos, signatures, and watermarks. {{FORBIDDEN}}
```

## Slot Guidance / 槽位说明

- `STRUCTURAL_SUBJECT`: one object or system supplied by the visual score.
- `CONCRETE_RELATION`: one path, gate, transfer, or operating relation.
- `WARM_COOL_BLOCKS`: hard-edged planes with semantic roles, not decoration.
- `CONTOUR_DENSITY`: dense enough for a plate, open enough to preserve hierarchy.

## Fidelity Gate / 保真门

- Subject and relation remain legible before print material is inspected.
- Hard-edged blocks organize meaning and do not become gradients or chrome.
- The same plate logic transfers to two ordinary, non-science-fiction topics.
- Removing “retro-future” leaves a coherent etched print method.
