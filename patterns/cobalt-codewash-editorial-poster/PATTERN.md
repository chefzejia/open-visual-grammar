## Definition / 定义

EN: Cobalt Codewash Editorial Poster builds one portrait editorial world from
four interacting systems: warm paper, one large cobalt print field, one
source-grounded subject reduced to line or plate structure, and one directional
stream of code-like glyphs. Typography occupies the same spatial argument. The
cobalt field is not decoration, the code is not cyber ambience, and the subject
is not a pasted photograph. Each element changes the reading path of the others.

CN：钴蓝代码洗编辑海报由四套互相作用的系统构成一个竖版编辑世界：暖纸面、一块大型钴蓝印刷色场、
一个被压缩为线描或版刻结构的来源主体，以及一条有方向的代码式字形流。文字也必须进入同一个空间论证。
钴蓝色场不是装饰，代码不是赛博氛围，主体也不是粘贴照片；每个元素都要改变其他元素的阅读路径。

This Pattern stores a transferable visual method. It does not store a named art
district, event title, building, speaker, slogan, handwriting specimen, or a
specific code fragment.

本 Pattern 保存可迁移的视觉方法，不保存具体艺术区、活动标题、建筑、演讲者、口号、手写字样或代码片段。

## Style Signature / 风格签名

High-fidelity output preserves all six signatures:

1. a warm ivory or lightly fibrous paper field remains visibly active;
2. one cobalt-blue dry-brush or screenprint mass owns roughly one third to two
   thirds of the page and enters from an edge;
3. one real or explicitly supplied subject is redrawn as sparse ink, etched
   contour, architectural linework, or restrained plate color;
4. a tapered stream of binary digits, punctuation, brackets, or short
   code-like marks forms one path between field, subject, and text;
5. editorial typography uses one large display block, one compact information
   block, and optional edge microcopy or one handwritten accent;
6. generous paper negative space separates reading tasks while field, subject,
   glyph stream, and type still share axes, edges, or directional force.

高保真结果必须同时保留六个签名：

1. 暖象牙色或带轻微纤维感的纸面持续作为主动背景；
2. 一块钴蓝干刷或丝网印刷色场从画面边缘进入，占页面约三分之一到三分之二；
3. 一个真实或明确提供的主体被重绘成稀疏墨线、蚀刻轮廓、建筑线稿或克制的版刻色层；
4. 二进制数字、标点、括号或短代码式符号形成一条有粗细与密度变化的单一路径，连接色场、主体与文字；
5. 编辑排版由一个大型展示标题、一个紧凑信息块，以及可选的边缘微文字或一条手写强调组成；
6. 大面积纸面留白分隔阅读任务，但色场、主体、字形流和文字仍共享轴线、边缘或方向力。

## Invariants / 不变量

- Use exactly one dominant cobalt field. It may contain pressure variation,
  transparent scraping, white dry-brush breaks, and rough edges, but it may not
  split into unrelated decorative blobs.
- The paper ground remains visible and materially quiet. Do not replace it with
  a flat digital beige, a global vintage filter, a gradient, or a dark scene.
- The source subject keeps three to five identity anchors before stylization.
  Architecture may retain roofline, chimney rhythm, façade spacing, material
  boundary, or one verified sign shape; another subject must declare equivalent
  anchors.
- Subject treatment is reductive: ink contour, etched line, graphite plate, or
  restrained source-derived color. Do not keep a rectangular photo window.
- Use one code stream with a clear origin, direction, density change, and
  destination. It must bridge structural regions or explain transformation;
  random code confetti fails.
- Code-like marks are graphical evidence, not technical claims. Unless supplied
  copy must be exact, avoid long readable programs, invented formulas, terminal
  windows, UI panels, circuit boards, or neon cyberpunk symbols.
- The palette is warm paper, one cobalt role, charcoal or black structure, and
  at most one muted source-derived accent such as brick, clay, rust, or skin.
- Typography follows the composition rather than sitting in a detached caption
  card. The display block and subject or cobalt field must share an axis,
  boundary, overlap, or deliberate negative shape.
- Edge microcopy is sparse and subordinate. A handwritten accent is optional,
  appears at most once, and cannot become the main font system.
- The final bitmap is generated in one model pass. A structure guide may supply
  grouping, exact copy, scale, reading order, and overlap, but it is not a final
  layer and cannot remain visible as scaffolding.
- Copy no named place, building, person, event, slogan, date, or code fragment
  from a visual reference unless the current brief independently supplies it.

- 只使用一块主导钴蓝色场。允许出现印压变化、透明刮擦、白色干刷断裂和粗糙边缘，但不能分裂成互不相关的装饰色块；
- 纸面必须可见且安静，不能被数字米色、全局复古滤镜、渐变或深色场景替代；
- 主体在风格化前保留三到五个身份锚点。建筑可保留屋顶线、烟囱节奏、立面间距、材质边界或一个已核实标记；其他主体需要声明等价锚点；
- 主体采用减法处理：墨线、蚀刻线、石墨版或克制的来源色，不保留矩形照片窗口；
- 只使用一条具有明确起点、方向、密度变化和终点的代码流。它必须连接结构区域或说明变化，随机撒代码不成立；
- 代码式符号是图形证据，不是技术事实。除非当前文案要求逐字准确，否则不要生成长段可读程序、虚构公式、终端窗口、UI 面板、电路板或霓虹赛博符号；
- 配色由暖纸色、一个钴蓝角色、炭黑结构色，以及最多一种来自主体的低饱和强调色组成；
- 文字服从构图，不能进入孤立说明卡。展示标题必须与主体或蓝色色场共享轴线、边界、遮挡或有意的负形；
- 边缘微文字保持稀疏和次要。手写强调最多出现一次，不能成为主字体系统；
- 正式位图由模型一次性生成。结构引导图可以传递分区、准确文案、尺度、阅读顺序和遮挡，但不能作为最终图层，也不能残留脚手架；
- 除非当前简报独立提供，不能从参考图复制具体地点、建筑、人物、活动、口号、日期或代码片段。

## Composition Families / 构图家族

### Ground Swell / 地面涌浪

A cobalt field rises from the lower edge as a sloped or curved landform. Place
the reduced subject on, beside, or partially inside its crest. The glyph stream
rises from the subject or field edge into the paper space. Keep the main title
in the upper paper field and reserve compact event information inside a calmer
part of the cobalt mass or along its boundary.

钴蓝色场从下缘以斜坡或弧形地貌涌入。线描主体位于浪脊上、旁边或局部嵌入其中；字形流从主体或色场边缘
升入纸面。主标题位于上方纸面，紧凑信息进入较平静的蓝色色块或其边界。

### Overhead Slab / 顶部压场

A broad cobalt slab enters from the upper edge and compresses the page. The
subject anchors the lower third. The glyph stream drips or curves from the blue
field toward the subject, making a visible transition from abstract system to
physical evidence. Keep one substantial paper corridor between them.

宽阔钴蓝色块从上缘压入页面，主体锚定下三分之一；字形流从蓝色色场滴落或弯向主体，把抽象系统与物理证据
连接起来。两者之间保留一条明显的纸面通道。

### Side Cliff / 侧壁断崖

The cobalt mass occupies the left or right edge as a vertical cliff with a
rough inward contour. Typography uses the opposite paper field. The reduced
subject sits near the lower meeting point, and the glyph stream crosses or
follows the paper corridor without closing it completely.

钴蓝色场占据左侧或右侧边缘，形成向内粗糙侵入的竖向断崖。文字使用另一侧纸面；主体位于下方交界点，
字形流穿过或跟随纸面通道，但不能把通道完全封死。

### Suspended Field / 悬浮色场

One irregular cobalt plate floats across the upper-middle page without touching
every edge. The title owns the paper above or beside it. The subject stays low
and small enough to preserve scale. The glyph stream is the only connector
between suspended field and grounded subject.

一块不规则钴蓝版面悬浮在页面中上部，不触及所有边缘。标题占据其上方或侧面的纸面；主体保持较低位置和较小尺度，
由字形流承担悬浮色场与落地主体之间唯一的连接。

Choose one family per artifact. The family controls the first read; do not
combine a bottom wave, two side cliffs, and a floating slab in one poster.

每张作品只选择一个构图家族。构图家族控制第一眼，不能同时加入底部涌浪、两侧断崖和悬浮色场。

## Fit / 适用范围

- art-and-technology events, research symposia, and experimental exhibitions;
- cultural or architectural subjects interpreted through computation, systems,
  data, interaction, or digital craft;
- editorial covers about translation between a physical subject and an
  abstract process;
- portrait-format event posters whose text can be reduced to a clear title,
  one information block, and limited metadata;
- unrelated topics that can supply one real anchor and one directional system,
  such as a climate-research vessel with sensor data or a biological specimen
  with a sequence trail.

适合艺术与技术活动、研究论坛、实验展览；通过计算、系统、数据、交互或数字工艺解释文化与建筑主体；
讨论物理主体和抽象过程之间转换的编辑封面；以及能够压缩为标题、一个信息块和有限元数据的竖版活动海报。
它也应迁移到无关题材，例如由传感器数据连接的气候研究船，或由序列轨迹连接的生物样本。

## Weak Fit / 不适合

- routine corporate conferences, startup gradients, or generic AI imagery;
- exact source photography, legal evidence, technical manuals, or code that
  must be read and executed;
- multi-speaker schedules, dense sponsor walls, or long copy that cannot be
  reduced into two or three information depths;
- full-color product advertising, glossy 3D campaigns, entertainment posters,
  or cyberpunk scenes;
- topics with no credible relation between a physical anchor and an abstract
  information path.

## Material, Density, And Temperature / 材质、密度与温度

The main material is cobalt dry-brush or imperfect screenprint on warm uncoated
paper. The subject uses a secondary etched, inked, or graphite plate language.
Density is intentionally uneven: the cobalt field may be visually dense, the
subject moderately detailed, the paper field open, and the glyph stream locally
concentrated before fading. The temperature is intelligent, tactile, humane,
and exploratory rather than futuristic, corporate, nostalgic, or theatrical.

主材质是暖色非涂布纸上的钴蓝干刷或不完美丝网印刷；主体使用次一级的蚀刻、墨线或石墨版语言。
密度有意不均：钴蓝色场可以浓重，主体保持中等细节，纸面开放，字形流局部聚集后逐渐消散。
情绪理性、有触感、有人味且带探索性，不做未来科技模板、企业宣传、怀旧或戏剧化。

## Text Strategy / 文字策略

Choose one strategy before writing the model prompt:

1. **Native concise editorial / 原生精简编辑文字:** use for one display title,
   one subtitle or theme line, and a small metadata block. Quote every required
   string verbatim and forbid all unsupported copy.
2. **Structure-guide native editorial / 结构引导原生编辑文字:** use when the
   poster requires several text regions. Supply a neutral guide containing
   exact copy, region bounds, reading order, relative scale, and planned
   overlaps. The model redraws the complete poster in one pass; the guide is not
   composited into the result.

Do not rescue incorrect text with HTML, SVG, Canvas, or later overlays. Reduce
the copy, strengthen the structure guide, and regenerate once. If exact dense
copy still fails, reject this Pattern for that artifact rather than storing a
weaker intermediate result.

选择一种文字策略：原生精简编辑文字适用于一个展示标题、一条副标题或主题句和一个小型元数据块；
结构引导原生编辑文字适用于多个文字区域，使用中性引导图提供逐字文案、区域边界、阅读顺序、相对尺度和计划遮挡。
最终成品仍由模型一次性重绘，不能把引导图叠加进结果。文字失败后不得用 HTML、SVG、Canvas 或后期叠字补救；
应减少文案、强化结构引导并只针对根因再生成一次。密集准确文案仍失败时，直接拒绝本 Pattern 在该任务中的适用性。

## Transfer Boundaries / 迁移边界

- `source-grounded-place-poster` owns source identity and verified place facts
  when the art direction may vary. Use this Pattern when cobalt print pressure,
  line reconstruction, and a directional glyph stream are essential to the
  requested visual identity.
- `limited-color-print-editorial` owns general paper-plus-one-ink reduction.
  This Pattern additionally requires a reconstructed evidence subject and one
  code-like spatial connector.
- `retro-pixel-halftone-tech-editorial` owns pixel or halftone machinery and
  dense retro-technical plates. This Pattern stays spacious, paper-led, and
  brush-led; it excludes dashboards, pixel grids, and machine panels.
- Transfer the relation among paper, cobalt field, subject plate, glyph stream,
  and typography. Do not transfer a reference building, district number,
  speaker, event wording, handwritten phrase, or literal program.
- Industrial architecture is one valid subject, not a permanent requirement.
  The Pattern must remain recognizable with a vessel, instrument, organism,
  landscape structure, or crafted object.

- 当来源身份和地点事实是首要约束、艺术方向可以变化时，使用 `source-grounded-place-poster`；当钴蓝印压、线描重构和方向性字形流是请求中的核心身份时，使用本 Pattern；
- `limited-color-print-editorial` 负责一般性的纸面加单墨色减法，本 Pattern 还必须包含一个重构的证据主体和一条代码式空间连接；
- `retro-pixel-halftone-tech-editorial` 负责像素、网点和密集复古技术版，本 Pattern 保持宽松、纸面主导和笔触主导，不引入仪表盘、像素网格或机器面板；
- 迁移纸面、钴蓝色场、主体版刻、字形流和文字之间的关系，不迁移参考图中的建筑、地区编号、人物、活动文案、手写短句或真实程序；
- 工业建筑只是一个有效主体，不是永久要求。换成船舶、仪器、生物体、地貌结构或手工器物后仍应可辨认。

## Failure Modes / 失败方式

- **Blue decoration:** cobalt paint is attractive but does not control reading
  order. Enlarge or reposition it until it owns one composition family.
- **Generic Swiss poster:** typography is clean but the subject, glyph stream,
  and print field are unrelated. Restore a shared axis and one directional
  transformation.
- **Photo window:** the source remains a rectangular photograph. Reconstruct it
  as line, etching, graphite, or a restrained plate while preserving anchors.
- **Code confetti:** digits and brackets scatter everywhere. Reduce them to one
  path with a declared origin and destination.
- **Cyberpunk drift:** neon, black backgrounds, circuitry, HUDs, glow, chrome,
  or cinematic light replace paper and print pressure. Remove them completely.
- **Reference leakage:** a district number, building, speaker, slogan, or
  handwriting from the reference appears. Remove the copied identity and keep
  only the transferable relation.
- **Text fog:** required copy is missing, substituted, or joined by pseudo-text.
  Reduce copy or rebuild the structure guide; do not overlay a correction.
- **Palette creep:** bright red, green, purple, or multiple blues become
  decoration. Restore one cobalt role and at most one muted source accent.
- **Dense brochure:** every paper region receives copy. Restore one display
  block, one compact information block, and active negative space.
- **Subjectless abstraction:** removing the title leaves only blue paint and
  code. Restore one evidence-bearing reconstructed subject or reject the route.

If these failures recur across unrelated topics, rewrite or delete this Pattern
instead of keeping a mood preset.

## Image QA / 图像验收

- Read the first message before inspecting pigment texture.
- Confirm one and only one composition family.
- Check the source identity anchors before judging style similarity.
- Trace the glyph stream from origin through density change to destination.
- Compare every required text string character by character.
- Confirm that cobalt remains one ink role and that any source accent is muted.
- Remove the title mentally: the subject, field, and glyph path must still form
  one spatial argument.
- Remove color mentally: mass, negative space, title hierarchy, subject anchor,
  and glyph direction must still distinguish the Pattern.
- Reject any output that requires a later text layer or that copies reference
  identity.
