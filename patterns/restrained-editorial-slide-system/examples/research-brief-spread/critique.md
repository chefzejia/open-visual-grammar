# Critique

This reviewed example belongs to `restrained-editorial-slide-system` because
one reading task controls the page: a visible signal becomes a better decision
after context is added.

What it teaches:

- the master grid is visible but quiet, with the same margins and baseline
  logic across headline, evidence, and footer;
- display type is scarce and carries the first read, while compact sans text
  handles labels and explanatory copy;
- the evidence crop is bounded, dark, and clearly labeled instead of becoming a
  full-page background;
- the muted rust accent marks the semantic hinge only;
- the synthetic source label prevents the image from implying unsupported data.

The SVG source is deterministic and the WebP is the reviewable delivery asset.
