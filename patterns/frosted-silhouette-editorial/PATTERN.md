# Frosted Silhouette Editorial

```yaml
id: frosted-silhouette-editorial
artifact_kinds:
  - image-cover
surfaces:
  - image-generation
when_to_use: Quiet editorial covers where one recognizable silhouette or relation can be organized through regional frosted windows, clean geometry, and controlled negative space.
avoid_when: Exact UI proof, dense tutorials, photographic evidence, playful game logic, or topics that need full-frame blur, glossy glass, or long text.
```

中文名：磨砂剪影编辑封面
English name: Frosted Silhouette Editorial


## Definition / 定义

EN: Frosted Silhouette Editorial compresses a subject's most recognizable
contour, structure, posture, or relation into a clean editorial composition.
Regional frosted windows then control what is revealed, softened, or layered.
The result is a quiet poster or cover with designed depth, not a generic glass
effect or a product UI mockup.

CN：磨砂剪影编辑封面把主体最有辨识度的轮廓、结构、姿态或关系压缩成干净的编辑构图，
再用区域化磨砂视窗控制清晰、柔焦和透叠。它是一种安静的海报或封面语法，形成的是有设计的
深度，不是通用玻璃特效，也不是产品 UI 预览图。

This Pattern was calibrated against public descriptions for `P072` and
`生成磨砂玻璃效果剪影艺术海报`.
Their names, text, and preview images are external evidence only; this Pattern
keeps the paraphrased visual method.

本 Pattern 参考了 `P072` 与“生成磨砂玻璃效果剪影艺术海报”描述进行校准。
其中的名称、文字和预览图只作外部证据；本 Pattern 只保留转述后的视觉方法。

## Invariants / 不变量

- One primary silhouette or relation stays identifiable before the frosted
  treatment is inspected.
- The outer composition is clean and geometric: stable margins, deliberate
  alignment, and a small number of planes or windows.
- Frosting is regional and semantic. Clear, softened, and translucent areas each
  explain a relation, hierarchy, or depth cue.
- Near-white or neutral space remains active and visible; the surface is not a
  full-frame blur or a dark glass panel.
- Flat color fields, fine lines, and controlled contrast carry the structure;
  depth is quiet and continuous rather than glossy or cinematic.
- Text is short, checked, and integrated as editorial type or reserved for
  deterministic typesetting.

- 一个主要剪影或关系在检查磨砂处理前就必须可辨；
- 外部构图干净且几何稳定：边距明确、对齐有意、视窗或平面数量受控；
- 磨砂必须分区且有语义，清晰、柔焦和透叠区域分别说明关系、层级或深度；
- 近白或中性空间保持主动可见，不能整幅平均模糊，也不能变成深色玻璃面板；
- 平面色块、细线和受控反差承担结构，深度安静连续，不做光亮或电影化；
- 文字短且经过核对，以编辑排版融入，或留给确定性排版。

## Style Signature / 风格签名

High-fidelity output preserves four signatures:

1. a recognizable contour or relation as the first read;
2. a stable geometric frame or page field;
3. regional frosted windows with different reveal levels;
4. restrained type and negative space that make the image feel exhibited or
   published rather than rendered as an effect demo.

高保真结果必须同时保留四个签名：

1. 可辨识的轮廓或关系作为第一眼信息；
2. 稳定的几何框架或页面场；
3. 具有不同显隐程度的区域化磨砂视窗；
4. 克制排版与主动留白，让画面像展览或出版物，而不是特效演示。

## Ownership Contract / 权责契约

The upstream visual argument owns the first read, visual action, main subject,
and concrete evidence. This Pattern owns the frame, alignment, frosted reveal
levels, palette restraint, density, typography hierarchy, and emotional
temperature. The runtime owns aspect ratio, text strategy, and export details.

上游视觉论证负责第一眼信息、视觉动作、主体与具体证据。本 Pattern 负责框架、对齐、磨砂显隐
层级、克制配色、密度、字体层级和情绪温度。Runtime 只负责画幅、文字策略和导出细节。

Do not let the topic compilation route introduce a new glass material, cinematic camera,
or interface system. The source relation must remain visible even when all
material language is removed.

主题 compilation route 不得再发明另一套玻璃材质、电影镜头或界面系统。即使移除所有材质词，来源关系仍
必须可见。

## Internal Layout Routes / 内部版式路由

### Reveal Ladder / 显隐阶梯

Use one silhouette or relation across two or three nested reveal levels: clear,
softened, and translucent. The changes must explain hierarchy, distance, or
cause; do not distribute blur evenly for decoration.

让一个剪影或关系经过两到三层显隐：清晰、柔焦和透叠。变化必须说明层级、距离或因果，不能
为了装饰平均分配模糊。

### Geometric Window / 几何视窗

Use a small number of aligned rectangles, arcs, or cut planes to frame the
subject. Each window may reveal a different part of the same relation, but the
outer grid stays quiet and stable.

用少量对齐的矩形、弧面或切面框住主体。每个视窗可以揭示同一关系的不同部分，但外部网格必须
安静稳定。

### Silhouette Field / 剪影场

Use one dominant contour floating in near-white space, with a few fine lines or
flat fields indicating the supplied action. Choose this route when the argument
needs calm focus rather than a dense poster.

让一个主导剪影悬浮在近白空间中，用少量细线或平面色块说明上游动作。只有在论证需要安静聚焦、
而不是高密度海报时使用。

## Fit / 适用范围

Recommended:

- quiet editorial covers with one precise relation;
- exhibition, architecture, object, or concept posters;
- topics where reveal, distance, obstruction, or partial access is the visual
  action;
- square or horizontal compositions with short type.

适合：

- 有一个精确关系的安静编辑封面；
- 展览、建筑、物件或概念海报；
- 视觉动作是显露、距离、遮挡或部分可见的主题；
- 使用短文字的方形或横向构图。

## Weak Fit / 不适合

- exact product screenshots or UI demonstrations;
- multi-step instructional diagrams;
- high-energy public arguments that need a strong poster collision;
- scenes whose meaning depends on realistic light, transparent material physics,
  or long copy.

- 精确产品截图或 UI 演示；
- 多步骤说明图；
- 需要强海报冲撞的高能公共论点；
- 依赖真实光学、透明材质物理或长篇文字的主题。

## Material, Density, And Temperature / 材质、密度与温度

Material is matte and controlled: paper white, neutral grey, one restrained
accent if needed, crisp geometric edges, subtle grain, and regional frosted
translucency. Avoid chrome, liquid glass, heavy bloom, or plastic highlights.

材质保持哑光和克制：纸白、中性灰、必要时的一种受控强调色、清晰几何边缘、细微颗粒和区域化
磨砂透叠。避免金属铬、液态玻璃、强光晕和塑料高光。

Density: low to medium. Every window, line, and label must clarify the same
relation. Emotional temperature: quiet, precise, clean, contemplative, and
slightly distant.

密度：低到中。每个视窗、线条和标注都必须说明同一个关系。情绪：安静、准确、洁净、沉思、带有
一点距离感。

## Transfer Boundaries / 迁移边界

- Unlike `minimal-handdrawn-linework`, this Pattern is geometric and frosted,
  not hand-corrected or ink-led.
- Unlike `retro-pixel-halftone-tech-editorial`, it is sparse and reveal-driven,
  not a dense one-bit broadsheet with proof modules.
- Unlike `semantic-concept-poster`, short text is not necessarily the main visual
  object; the reveal relation carries the argument.
- Unlike a glassmorphism UI, windows are editorial evidence and do not form
  buttons, dashboards, or a product interface.

- 与 `minimal-handdrawn-linework` 不同，本 Pattern 是几何和磨砂驱动，不是手绘修正或墨线驱动；
- 与 `retro-pixel-halftone-tech-editorial` 不同，本 Pattern 稀疏且由显隐关系驱动，不是高密度一比特大报；
- 与 `semantic-concept-poster` 不同，短文字不一定是主视觉对象，显隐关系才承载论证；
- 与玻璃拟态 UI 不同，视窗是编辑证据，不构成按钮、仪表盘或产品界面。

## Failure Modes / 失败方式

- Frosting is applied uniformly and loses semantic reveal levels.
- The silhouette becomes an unrecognizable abstract blob.
- Glass material turns glossy, dark, chrome-like, or cinematic.
- Windows become a dashboard, UI mockup, or decorative grid.
- The topic compilation route adds unrelated objects instead of clarifying the supplied
  action.
- Text becomes long, pseudo-Chinese, or a generic title pasted in empty space.
- Negative space disappears under gradients, bloom, or dense decoration.

- 磨砂均匀铺满，显隐层级失去语义；
- 剪影变成无法辨认的抽象团块；
- 玻璃材质变得光亮、深黑、铬金属化或电影化；
- 视窗退化成仪表盘、UI mockup 或装饰网格；
- 主题 compilation route 添加无关物件，而不是说明上游动作；
- 文字变成长段、伪中文，或贴在空白处的通用标题；
- 渐变、光晕和密集装饰吞掉留白。

## Fidelity Gate / 保真门

1. Can the contour or relation be named before the frosted treatment is noticed?
2. Do clear, softened, and translucent regions each have a reason?
3. Does the outer geometry remain stable at thumbnail size?
4. Is the material matte and restrained rather than a glass-effect demo?
5. After removing color and translucency words, does the same reveal structure
   remain?

1. 不看磨砂材质，是否已经能说出主体轮廓或关系？
2. 清晰、柔焦和透叠区域是否各自有原因？
3. 缩小后外部几何是否仍然稳定？
4. 材质是否哑光克制，而不是玻璃特效演示？
5. 去掉配色和透叠词后，显隐结构是否仍然存在？

# Image-Generation Meta-Prompt / 图像生成元提示词

This compilation route compiles the `frosted-silhouette-editorial` Pattern for image
generation. It is calibrated by documented method descriptions for `P072` and the
frosted-silhouette poster skill, not by copying their prompts or preview images.

```text
Create {{ARTIFACT_KIND}} for {{AUDIENCE}} at {{ASPECT_RATIO}}.

The upstream visual argument is authoritative:
- First read: {{FIRST_READ}}
- Visual action: {{VISUAL_ACTION}}
- Main subject: {{MAIN_SUBJECT}}
- Concrete evidence: {{CONCRETE_EVIDENCE}}
- Text handling: {{TEXT_HANDLING}}

Use the Frosted Silhouette Editorial visual grammar. Preserve the supplied
subject, action, and evidence. First reduce {{MAIN_SUBJECT}} to one recognizable
contour, posture, structure, or relation. Choose Reveal Ladder, Geometric Window,
or Silhouette Field according to the visual argument; do not invent a second art
direction.

Build a clean geometric page with stable margins and near-white or neutral space.
Use two or three regional reveal levels—clear, softened, and translucent—only on
{{REVEAL_TARGETS}}. Each window must explain hierarchy, distance, obstruction,
or access. Keep the silhouette recognizable before and after the reveal treatment.

Use matte paper or neutral surfaces, crisp flat edges, fine lines, subtle grain,
and at most one restrained accent color. Keep density low to medium. Treat short
text as integrated editorial type or leave a clean region for deterministic
typesetting. The emotional temperature is quiet, precise, clean, contemplative,
and slightly distant.

Avoid uniform blur, dark glass panels, chrome, liquid glass, glossy highlights,
cinematic bloom, gradients, dashboards, buttons, product UI, dense diagrams,
unrelated objects, long generated text, pseudo-Chinese, logos, signatures, and
watermarks. Do not copy any reference composition, subject, slogan, or exact
coordinates. {{FORBIDDEN}}
```

## Slot Guidance / 槽位说明

- `REVEAL_TARGETS`: name one to three semantic parts that should be clear,
  softened, or translucent. Do not turn it into a color or blur budget.
- `TEXT_HANDLING`: use `short checked type`, `blank region for deterministic
  typesetting`, or a mixed strategy.
- `FORBIDDEN`: add only topic-specific mistakes; the Pattern's material and UI
  prohibitions already apply.

- `REVEAL_TARGETS`：写出一到三个需要清晰、柔焦或透叠的语义部位，不要把它写成颜色或模糊预算；
- `TEXT_HANDLING`：选择“短且经过核对的文字”“为确定性排版留空”或混合策略；
- `FORBIDDEN`：只补充主题特有错误，Pattern 已有的材质和 UI 禁止项不必重复。

## Stable Checks / 稳定检查

- One recognizable silhouette or relation remains identifiable before frosting.
- Clear, softened, and translucent regions are semantic reveal levels, not a
  uniform blur or glossy glassmorphism surface.
- Stable margins, a small number of geometric windows, active near-white space,
  and short checked text keep the artifact editorial and quiet.
- The method must transfer to an archive reveal and a partially obscured public
  notice without becoming a dashboard, UI mockup, or cinematic glass scene.

## Failure Recovery / 失败恢复

If frosting hides the subject, windows become decorative grids, or gradients and
bloom consume the field, restore the reveal ladder and delete unsupported
objects. Repeated loss of the relation is grounds to delete the Pattern.
